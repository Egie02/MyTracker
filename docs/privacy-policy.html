MTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MTracker ("the app") is a personal money tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles on your device.

Summary

  • All financial data stays on your device (local SQLite database).
  • We do not operate a cloud server or collect your transactions.
  • Optional export to XLSX is initiated by you and shared via your device's share sheet.

Data stored on your device

  • Transactions, accounts, categories, and balances
  • A hashed PIN and optional biometric unlock preference (via secure storage)
  • Theme and UI preferences

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Biometric / fingerprintOptional unlock with Face ID or fingerprint
Storage / filesExport and import XLSX backups you choose

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration with us
  • No analytics SDK in the default build
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Your choices

  • Export data anytime from Settings
  • Clear all data removes the local database and PIN from the device
  • Uninstalling the app removes local data unless you exported a backup

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
